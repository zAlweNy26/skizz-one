## Play

- Take turns drawing a word while everyone else races to guess it in the chat.
- The drawer picks one of three words and can swap the choices once per turn.
- Points reward quick guesses and good drawings, and a game never ends in a tie.
- A podium and end-of-game awards: "Fastest guess", "Most liked drawing", "Almost had it" and "Picasso".
- Guessers can like or dislike the drawing, and players who already guessed chat privately.

## Rooms

- Create a room and share its link, or join one with its code.
- Public rooms are listed on the home page, and "Quick play" drops you into one.
- The host sets the drawing time, rounds, hints, word language and custom words.
- Everyone can vote to pause the game or to kick a player out.
- A dropped player keeps their seat for 30 seconds.

## Drawing

- Every stroke streams to the other players as it is drawn.
- Brush, eraser and paint bucket, with undo, redo and clear.
- Download the drawing as SVG between turns.

## Everywhere

- Built for phones first, with a landscape layout, and installable as an app.
- English and Italian, for both the interface and the words.
- Sounds for the big moments of the game.
