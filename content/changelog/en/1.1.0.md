## New

- Roll a new random avatar without changing your name.
- A credits page for the sounds and special thanks.

## Fixes

- Game sounds now play on iPhone and iPad, even with the silent switch on.
- Drawing with a finger no longer scrolls the page.
- The paint bucket can fill the blank canvas and areas open against its edge.
- The podium and awards fit on small screens.
- Like and dislike buttons match the drawer's badges on desktop.

## Security

- Players can no longer take over another player's seat to see the word.
- Drawings are cleaned before they are shown to other players.
- Guesses, chat and game actions are rate limited.
