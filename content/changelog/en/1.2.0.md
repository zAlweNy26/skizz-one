## New

- Drawing rules: the host can turn off undo or the eraser, or limit each turn to a few random colours.
- Chaos mode: every turn gets one random rule, announced in the chat.
- Active rules show in the lobby and next to the timer, so guessers know why the banana is blue.
- Show a QR code of the room so friends can join from their phone, handy on a shared screen.
- New rooms pick their word language from your browser, and fall back to English.

## Improvements

- Game settings are split into Game, Words and Rules tabs.
- The lobby buttons are smaller on phones and fit on one row.
