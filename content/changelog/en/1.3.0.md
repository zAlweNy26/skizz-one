## New

- French, German and Spanish word lists, about 300 words each.
- A public "Stats" page: games played, rematches, how turns end and the hardest and easiest words.
- "Quick play" drops you into a random open room, or starts a new one when none is free. It replaces the list of public rooms.
- When a new version is out, an old tab asks you to reload instead of playing out of sync.

## Improvements

- A room opens once it's fully loaded, instead of filling in piece by piece.
- The "Create a room" and "Quick play" buttons show they're working, so a double tap doesn't open two rooms.
- Guesses match `ß` with `ss` and `œ` with `oe`, and read hyphens and apostrophes as spaces.
- Offensive player names are refused.
- A translated page for links that lead nowhere.

## Fixes

- Pressing E picks the partial eraser, like clicking it does.

## Security

- Stricter security headers on every page.
- The "Terms of service" now explain the anonymous game statistics and error logs.
