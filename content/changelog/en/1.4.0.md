## New

- Pick a "Name colour" before joining: it shows on your name in the chat and the player list.
- About 150 harder words in each language.

## Improvements

- The drawer now earns 150% of the guessers' average, up from 90%.
- You can keep liking a drawing during the break after the turn, and those likes count towards "Most liked drawing".
- Turns change over more smoothly, with less work when a turn ends.

## Fixes

- The drawing toolbar's icons show up again instead of staying blank.
- A message you're typing is no longer cleared when a turn ends.
- The canvas stays big enough to draw on with small laptop screens.
