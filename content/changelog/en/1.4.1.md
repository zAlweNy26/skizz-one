## Improvements

- The countdown follows the room's clock, so it shows the same time on every device even when one's clock is off.
- Players who join during the break after a turn see the word, and can use "Download drawing" with the word as the file name.
- Screen readers spell out the word to guess, naming each blank and every letter a hint reveals.
- The "Pause" button stays visible but is greyed out while the game waits for a drawer who dropped out.
- Private rooms hold up to 20 players.
- Long games stay smooth: the chat keeps its latest 200 lines on screen.
- The name and room code fields have more room on phones.

## Fixes

- Kicking a player during a pause no longer leaves the following turns stuck as paused.
- Players whose connection was cut by an update no longer stay in the room for good.
- A drawer who reconnects after their turn no longer sees their old word on the next one.
- Pressing Enter with a broken invite link shows the room code error instead of opening an invalid room.
- "Played again" on the "Stats" page can no longer go over 100%.
- Words typed by hosts no longer appear on the "Stats" page.
