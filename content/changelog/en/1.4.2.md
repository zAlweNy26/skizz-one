## New

- Play in "Full screen" from the room's header, or from the menu on phones, to keep the game in focus. It isn't offered where the browser doesn't allow it, as on iPhone.

## Improvements

- A long word to guess shrinks to fit on one line instead of breaking in the middle. A phrase only wraps between its words.
