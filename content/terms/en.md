Don't share anything that is illegal, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit or graphically violent, or that infringes someone else's rights. Public rooms can be joined by anyone, so if you want to play only with people you know, use a private room and share its link.

## Reporting and removal

Players in a room can vote to remove someone who breaks these rules. If you come across content that breaks them, leave the room and report it through the contact below. The author may remove content, close rooms or block access at any time, without notice.

## Your data

SkizzOne never asks for an email address or a password. Your name, a random player ID and your preferences are saved in your browser. Drawings, chat messages and scores stay on the game server only while the room is in use and are deleted once everyone has left. Avatars are generated by DiceBear from your name, and the game is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver it. To improve the game, it keeps anonymous statistics about games, such as how many start and finish, their settings and which words were guessed, and logs errors from your browser along with the page and browser version. None of it includes your name, player ID or messages.
